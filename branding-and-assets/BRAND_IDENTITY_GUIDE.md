# BRAND IDENTITY & DESIGN SPECIFICATION
## IRON & LEAF CULTIVATION CO. &bull; OUTLAW BOTANICALS M.C.

---

### 1. Brand Concept & Strategic Positioning
**Iron & Leaf Cultivation Co.** merges two fiercely dedicated American subcultures: the brotherhood and craftsmanship of **outlaw motorcycle culture**, and the artisan precision of **local micro-batch organic cannabis cultivation**.

- **Core Tagline:** *"Born on the Road. Grown in the Dirt."*
- **Secondary Tagline:** *"High Octane Terpenes. Zero Corporate Bullshit."*
- **Brand Archetype:** The Rebel / Outlaw Craftsman (Rugged, Uncompromising, Loyal, High-Assurance).

---

### 2. Visual Identity & Logo System
The primary logo is inspired by traditional three-piece motorcycle club rocker back patches, fused with botanical craft elements:

1. **Top Rocker:** Arched bold typography reading **`IRON & LEAF`**.
2. **Bottom Rocker:** Inverse arched banner reading **`CULTIVATION CO.`**.
3. **Central Iconography:**
   - Outlaw biker skull fitted with vintage aviator riding goggles reflecting vivid emerald resin lights.
   - Crossed motorcycle engine pistons / exhaust manifolds symbolizing mechanical precision.
   - Symmetrical 7-finger cannabis fan leaf radiating behind the skull, representing living organic soil cultivation.
4. **Side Badges:**
   - Left: `CRAFT GROW`
   - Right: `MC` (Motor Club)
5. **Base Seal:** `OUTLAW BOTANICALS • 21+ ONLY`

### 4. Typography Hierarchy

1. **Headlines & Display:**
   - **Font:** `Bebas Neue` / `Oswald` (Google Fonts)
   - **Characteristics:** All-Caps, condensed, heavy visual punch, 2px letter-spacing.
2. **Serif & Heritage Titles:**
   - **Font:** `Cinzel` (Google Fonts)
   - **Characteristics:** Classical strength, regal authority, used for badges & certificates.
3. **Body & Interface Text:**
   - **Font:** `Inter` (Google Fonts)
   - **Characteristics:** Clean grotesque, exceptional legibility on OLED dark displays.

### 5. Packaging & Physical Merchandise Applications
- **Heavy Glass Curing Jars:** Matte black UV-coated glass with embossed metallic amber badge and batch tear-seal.
- **Mylar Smell-Proof Bags:** Satin gunmetal finish with neon emerald spot-UV varnish over the piston/leaf crest.
- **Apparel (The Cut):** Embroidered twill back patches for club members, screen-printed heavy cotton work shirts.
- **Wholesale Dispensary Displays:** Reclaimed barn wood and black welded iron pipe display cases.
