THE OUTLAW METHOD

Corporate cannabis cuts corners. We do not. Our brotherhood applies military discipline and mechanical precision to craft horticulture.

01

Heritage Clone & Landrace Cuts

We source authentic genetic cuts from underground breeders, veterans, and motorcycle runs dating back thirty years.

02

No-Till Living Organic Soil

Our plants feed on rich worm castings, mycorrhizae, kelp, and volcanic rock dust. Pure nature, maximum terpene expression.
